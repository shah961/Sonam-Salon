Privacy Policy

Transparency and information privacy at Sanam Salon.

Note: This information is provided for general website use and should be reviewed and finalized by the business owner before publication.

1. Information We Collect

Sanam Salon ("we", "our") respects your privacy. When you interact with our website, you may voluntarily submit personal information (such as your name, phone number, and preferred appointment timing) strictly through our website enquiry form or by calling us directly.

2. Use of Information

Any information voluntarily provided by you is strictly used for:

  • Responding to your appointment or service enquiries.
  • Contacting you regarding scheduled appointments or schedule modifications.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your contact information to third parties.

3. Payment & User Accounts

This website does not collect payments, process credit card transactions, or maintain user accounts online. All billing is handled in person at our physical location.

4. External Services & Images

This website loads optimized remote placeholder images and external CDNs (such as Google Fonts and GSAP libraries) to ensure optimal speed. These external providers may collect standard technical metadata (e.g., IP address, browser type) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies or performance profiling tools on this static website.

6. Data Security

We take standard, reasonable measures to keep your provided contact details confidential within salon administrative records.
